I build digital worlds for a living, but I live in a very real one filled with music, books, food, sports, and people I care about. My work has one obsession: make the user feel something.

Mob Town is a 3D taxi tycoon where city simulation meets real-world data. Built in Unity, the game combines driving mechanics, passenger AI, vehicle upgrades, day/night systems, and live weather integration to create a playful urban mobility experience.

Unity • AR • Mobile • Rhythm

Billy Shoulder Dance

Billy Shoulder Dance is an augmented reality rhythm game for Android. I worked as one of the programmers, focusing on AR image recognition with Vuforia, camera management, gameplay controllers, VFX implementation, and general game programming. I also contributed to the game design and overall interaction flow. The project won the Popular Game Award at Semana Atômica Unisinos 2018.
